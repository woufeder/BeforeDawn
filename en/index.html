Anyway Before Dawn

This is a message for you—and just as much, one I leave for myself.
Life is but a fleeting drift, crossing silent valleys, passing through desolation, wandering in circles, only to return to where it all began.

Though I do not know why I have come to rest here, still cannot find the direction to depart. Yet I hope that, at least for a fleeting moment, within my story, you might glimpse something that feels strangely familiar.

And before the break of dawn, may that feeling become a cradle—to hold the sorrow of both you and me.

Something in the distance seems to have caught your eye.
Where would you like to go next?
