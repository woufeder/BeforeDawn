Originals

They had names, and thus they had life.

Beneath the boundless stars, day and night become their doors, and rain and mist as windows.
They pass through first light, and through the withering of flowers and leaves.

Their souls turn into foam along the shore, settling as dust upon the ocean floor,
living on in poets’ songs, etched into the words exchanged between people.

This is their world, their ocean
